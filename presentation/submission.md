# Submission draft (P2)

- **Project name:** Kiln Watch
- **Challenge:** Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots (NASA Space Apps 2026)
- **Team:** Team Off The Binary: Arna (Frontend), Sajid (Backend), Aalvee (AI Agent), Ahnaf (Data), Nazifa (Research), Maruf (Design)
- **High-level summary:** In 2012 NASA's sharper VIIRS camera joined MODIS and recorded fire jumped, though nothing new was burning. Kiln Watch harmonizes the two into one record from 2003 to today and turns it into a burning-activity calendar for an area: its normal season, unusual days, critical weeks and a backtested two-week outlook, updated daily from NASA FIRMS. After correction the fake 2012 jump falls from 55.1 to 1.6 units (97% was the camera), the cameras agree (VIIRS/MODIS 4.4× → 1.04×, concordance 0.62 → 0.98), and the corrected value matches what MODIS saw directly (23.4 vs 23.7). An AI agent, Ask Kiln Watch, answers plain-language questions by choosing and running the project's own functions, and blocks any number they did not return. Two extensions: crop burning split into the Aman and Boro harvest windows, and the brick-kiln season read from NASA Black Marble night lights (checked with Sentinel-1 radar), because fire satellites cannot see enclosed kilns. Bangladesh is the first region and is live; South Asia is in progress.
- **Link to project repository:** https://github.com/Aalvee-Aarham/kiln-watch
- **Link to final project (site):** https://aalvee-aarham.github.io/kiln-watch/
- **NASA data used:** FIRMS MODIS C6.1 and VIIRS 375 m (S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) SP and NRT; MOD14A1/MYD14A1/VNP14A1 daily fire masks; Black Marble VNP46A2 night lights; ECOSTRESS L2T LSTE v2 and Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 surface temperature (tested for kilns, reported negative). All imagery via Google Earth Engine.
- **Space agency partner data:** Copernicus Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-5P TROPOMI (ESA); ERA5-Land (ECMWF)
- **Other data:** APAD kiln inventory (CC BY 4.0), OCHA HDX boundaries (CC BY-IGO), OpenAQ (CC BY 4.0)
- **Use of AI:** Code written with an AI coding assistant (Claude) under human direction. The original analysis decisions were pre-registered by the team. Amendment 1 (kiln activity from night lights and radar) was proposed, pre-specified and run with the AI assistant at the team's request. Inside the product, Ask Kiln Watch uses Claude to choose and explain the project's functions; it never computes a number. Details: `docs/AI_USE.md`.
